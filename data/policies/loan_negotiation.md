# Loan Rate Negotiation Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines how much flexibility a banking agent (human or AI) has to
discount a personal or auto loan interest rate during a customer conversation,
without requiring manager approval.

## Credit bands
Customers are placed into a credit band based on their credit score, which sets
the base interest rate before any discount is applied. Higher scores get lower
base rates.

## Relationship tier discounts
Customers in higher relationship tiers (Platinum, Gold, Silver, Standard) — based
on tenure, total relationship value, and product holding — are eligible for larger
discounts off the base rate. This rewards loyalty and higher-value relationships.

## Loyalty bonus
Customers who have banked with us for 5+ or 10+ years receive an additional
discount allowance on top of their tier discount, recognizing long-term loyalty.
This stacks with the tier discount but is still bounded by the absolute floor.

## Absolute floor
No matter how the tier discount and loyalty bonus stack, an agent may never
discount more than 200 basis points (2.00%) off the base rate without escalating
to a human underwriter. This protects margin and ensures pricing consistency.

## Competitor matching
If a customer says they have a better offer elsewhere, the agent may match it —
but only if matching stays within the absolute floor above. If matching the
competitor's rate would require exceeding the floor, the agent should not agree
on the spot; this must be escalated with the competitor offer details attached.

## Escalation triggers
A case must be escalated to a human loan officer if any of the following are true:
- The discount needed exceeds 200 basis points.
- The customer's credit score is below 580.
- The customer has an active delinquency on any account.
- The loan amount requested exceeds the standard underwriting threshold.

# Customer Retention Offer Policy

## Purpose
Defines how much retention credit — waived fees, bonus cash, account
credits — an agent may offer a customer who says they are considering
leaving the bank entirely, as opposed to negotiating a single product like
a loan rate or one fee.

## Tier-based caps
The maximum combined value of a retention offer scales with relationship
tier: Platinum customers can be offered the most, Standard tier the least,
reflecting the greater cost of losing a higher-value relationship.

## Eligibility
A customer must have been with the bank at least 12 months to qualify for
an agent-level retention offer. Newer relationships don't yet have the
track record to justify a discretionary credit without review.

## Escalation triggers
Escalate to a human relationship manager if the requested or needed
retention credit exceeds the tier cap, the customer's tenure is under 12
months, the customer has an active delinquency, or the customer is closing
every account they hold (a full exit) rather than just one product —
full-exit cases carry a different retention strategy that a human should
own.

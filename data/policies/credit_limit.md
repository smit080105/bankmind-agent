# Credit Limit / Overdraft Increase Policy

## Purpose
Defines how much an agent may temporarily or permanently increase a customer's
credit limit or overdraft facility without a human review.

## Eligibility
Before any increase is considered, the account must be at least 6 months old,
the customer's credit score must be at least 650, current utilization must be
80% or below, and there must be no missed payments in the last 6 months. If any
of these fail, this is not something the agent can approve on the spot.

## Tier-based caps
Temporary increases (30-day) are capped as a percentage of the current limit,
higher for higher tiers. Permanent increases follow a similar but more
conservative scale, since they carry longer-term risk.

## Escalation triggers
Escalate whenever eligibility requirements are not met, the requested increase
exceeds the tier's maximum percentage, or the customer has an active
delinquency anywhere in their relationship with the bank.
